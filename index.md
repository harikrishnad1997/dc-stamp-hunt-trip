## How stamps work here {#how-stamps-work}

- Many DC stamps are grouped by bookstore, not by memorial — ask for the full set at each desk. Lincoln bookstore trailer holds Lincoln, Korean War, Vietnam, and Constitution Gardens. Washington Monument Lodge holds National Mall, Washington Monument, and World War I Memorial. FDR bookstore holds FDR + World War II. Ford's Theatre bookstore holds Ford's + Pennsylvania Avenue NHS.
- If you don't see a stamp, ask a ranger. Carry loose paper as backup — some kiosks run out of ink or move the stamp.
- **America250 stamps:** NPS lists special America250 cancellation stamps at several Mall bookstores in 2026 — ask at each bookstore.
- **[B] = official NPS brochure pickup:** stops marked [B] have the free official park brochure (unigrid) at the desk named there — grab it where you get the stamp. Full pickup list is in the checklists below.
- 📍 Each stop below links to its Google Maps location.

**The stops, in order:**

- **8:30 AM — [Lincoln Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lincoln+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Start early before crowds. The memorial is open 24 hours; the bookstore trailer 🛍️ opens at 10:00 AM, so the Lincoln / Korean War / Vietnam / Constitution Gardens stamps come from a short backtrack here at 10:00, from the Korean War Memorial. [B] Brochures for Lincoln + Korean War + Vietnam are at this desk / the Mall information kiosks — collect all three here. Lincoln, Korean War, Vietnam, and Constitution Gardens stamps are all at this one desk. Quick look at the Vietnam Women's Memorial nearby.
- **9:15 AM — [Vietnam Veterans Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Vietnam+Veterans+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** The Wall + Three Soldiers, on the north side of the Reflecting Pool. Stamp already collected at the Lincoln desk. Visiting it before Korean War is the point of the reorder — no more criss-crossing the pool.
- **9:45 AM — [Korean War Veterans Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Korean+War+Veterans+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Cross to the south side of the pool once and keep moving east. ~15 min here — at 10:00, walk back to the Lincoln bookstore trailer for the Lincoln / Korean War / Vietnam / Constitution Gardens stamps, then on to the DC War Memorial. 📸 Entrance stone carved: “OUR NATION HONORS HER SONS AND DAUGHTERS WHO ANSWERED THE CALL TO DEFEND A COUNTRY THEY NEVER KNEW AND A PEOPLE THEY NEVER MET / 1950 • KOREA • 1953”.
- **~10:23 AM — [DC War Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=DC+War+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** No desk here — per NPS's Mall stamp page, its stamp is at the **MLK Jr. Memorial Bookstore** 🛍️, two stops from now instead of three hours later. ~12 min here — trimmed to absorb the Lincoln stamp backtrack. Don't confuse it with the World War I Memorial (Pershing Park) stamp, which is at the Lodge / FDR bookstore.
- **10:35 AM — [World War II Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=World+War+II+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** The WWII memorial at the east end of the Reflecting Pool (17th St), on the way from the DC War Memorial toward the Tidal Basin. Free, open 24 hours. No stamp desk here — collect the WWII Memorial stamp at the FDR bookstore, two stops from now (it also stocks the WWII Memorial brochure — ask there). 📸 Curved stone information pavilion engraved “WORLD WAR II MEMORIAL” / “NATIONAL PARK SERVICE INFORMATION”.
- **11:00 AM — [Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Martin+Luther+King+Jr+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Bookstore stamp 🛍️ — pick up the DC War Memorial stamp here. [B] Brochure at the bookstore. 📸 Official NPS sign at the entrance, then the Mountain of Despair stone entrance. Per NPS, this bookstore also holds the African American Civil War Memorial stamp (backup for Day 2). From here, walk the Tidal Basin path south.
- **11:30 AM — [Franklin D. Roosevelt Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Franklin+Delano+Roosevelt+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Bookstore stamp 🛍️ for FDR + World War II Memorial. [B] FDR brochure here — also ask for the World War II Memorial brochure. The four outdoor rooms are the longest stop of Day 1. 📸 Granite entrance wall inscribed “FRANKLIN DELANO ROOSEVELT / PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES / 1933–1945”.
- **12:10 PM — [Jefferson Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Thomas+Jefferson+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Bookstore stamp 🛍️. [B] Brochure at the bookstore. Midday in the new order — the sunset slot is traded for the shorter walk.
- **12:30 PM — [Washington Monument](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Washington+Monument+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (booked).** Fri Nov 20, 12:30–1:00 PM entry, 2 tickets. Stamp at the Lodge desk on arrival (~12:15) — National Mall, Washington Monument, and WWI Memorial stamps. 🛍️ [B] Main Day 1 brochure rack. Arrive at screening by 12:15; have ID + QR tickets ready (screenshot them — limited cell service). Out ~1:15–1:30 after the 45–60 min visit. Food trucks on the Monument grounds for lunch.
- **1:30 PM — [World War I Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=World+War+I+Memorial+Pershing+Park+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (Pershing Park).** A short walk north from the Monument grounds. Free, open daily. No desk here — its passport stamp is already in hand from the Lodge desk at the Monument (last stop). From here, walk east to the Eisenhower Memorial for the 1:55 stop. 📸 Entrance wall inscribed “WORLD WAR I MEMORIAL”.
- **1:55 PM — [Dwight D. Eisenhower Memorial](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dwight+D+Eisenhower+Memorial+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.** Own bookstore 🛍️ + own stamp. [B] Brochure at the bookstore. Per NPS, this bookstore also holds the **Belmont-Paul** stamp (Day 3 site) and the National Mall and Memorial Parks stamp — handy backups. From here, Metro L'Enfant Plaza → Capitol South, arriving at the Capitol Visitor Center at ~2:50 for the 3 PM tour. 📸 Low marble wall carved “DWIGHT D EISENHOWER”.

- **3:00 PM — [U.S. Capitol Tour](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=US+Capitol+Visitor+Center+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (booked).** Free guided tour — arrive at the Capitol Visitor Center (First St & East Capitol St, Capitol South Metro) at ~2:50 for the 3 PM tour. Covers the Crypt, Rotunda, and National Statuary Hall (~1 hr); does not include the House/Senate galleries. Not an NPS passport site, so no stamp here. Tour ends ~4 PM; then Metro/cab to the National Archives for the 4:45 PM entry (a cab is the safer bet for the 4:30 arrival).
- **4:45 PM — [National Archives Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=National+Archives+Museum+Washington+DC){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (booked, Rotunda entry).** Arrive by 4:30 and use the pre-ticketed line on Constitution Ave (701 Constitution Ave NW) — not the walk-up line. The ticket time is for Rotunda entry (Declaration of Independence, Constitution, Bill of Rights); plan 20–30 min there. Museum closes 5:30 PM, so this is a Rotunda-focused visit, not the full 90-min interactive experience. Then dinner, then the night lights walk.
- **~7:30 PM — Night lights walk (evening, free).** The Mall + Tidal Basin memorials, lit up. No ticket, no schedule. Metro to Foggy Bottom, then Lincoln → Vietnam → Korean War → DC War → MLK → FDR → Jefferson (~1.8 miles, ~1.5 hrs with photo stops), Metro home from Smithsonian. The memorials are open 24 hours and the area is patrolled; stick to the well-lit paths — the Tidal Basin stretches between memorials are quieter and darker than the Mall proper. This is the free version of the moonlight trolley tour, and Jefferson lit at night makes up for the sunset slot the v15 reorder gave up. Done by ~9:30 PM.

> **Day 1 walking note:** About 3.5–4 miles on foot, mostly flat, all in one direction — west to east, with one short north–south detour to Pershing Park for the WWI Memorial after the Monument. No car needed — Metro to Foggy Bottom to start; L'Enfant Plaza is closest at the Eisenhower finish. Evening adds ~1.8 miles for the night lights walk.
