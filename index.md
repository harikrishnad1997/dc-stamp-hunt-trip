## How stamps work here {#how-stamps-work}

- Many DC stamps are grouped by bookstore, not by memorial — ask for the full set at each desk. Lincoln bookstore trailer holds Lincoln, Korean War, Vietnam, and Constitution Gardens. Washington Monument Lodge holds National Mall, Washington Monument, and World War I Memorial. FDR bookstore holds FDR + World War II. Ford's Theatre bookstore holds Ford's + Pennsylvania Avenue NHS.
- If you don't see a stamp, ask a ranger. Carry loose paper as backup — some kiosks run out of ink or move the stamp.
- **America250 stamps:** NPS lists special America250 cancellation stamps at several Mall bookstores in 2026 — ask at each bookstore.
- **[B] = official NPS brochure pickup:** stops marked [B] have the free official park brochure (unigrid) at the desk named there — grab it where you get the stamp. Full pickup list is in the checklists below.
- 📍 Each stop below links to its Google Maps location.

Grounds open 6 AM–10 PM. **Parking: FREE** — Columbia Island Marina lot (leave the car here for the Pentagon Memorial walk too). **Stamp: there is NO stamp desk here** — but the Arlington House bookstore, two stops from now, carries the parkway selection including LBJ Grove, so you still collect it today.

Free, open 24 hours. ~15 min there, then walk back. (Pentagon interior tours are weekday-only with advance reservations; photos allowed only inside the memorial.)

Walk up ~15 min to **Arlington House** (NPS; 9:30 AM–4:30 PM, free). [B] Brochure at the bookstore. **The bookstore is the prize:** its GW Parkway stamp selection includes the **LBJ Grove stamp** — the one the grove itself can't give you — plus Arlington House and Theodore Roosevelt Island. Bookstore 9 AM–4:30 PM but closes a half-hour for lunch; if it's shut, do the house first. Then the JFK gravesite / Eternal Flame below the house, and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier — in winter the guard changes on the hour (12 PM today). Out by ~12:15 PM.
- **12:30 PM — [C&O Canal, Georgetown Visitor Center](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chesapeake+and+Ohio+Canal+Georgetown+Visitor+Center+1057+Thomas+Jefferson+St+NW+Washington+DC).** [B] Brochure at the desk, same desk as the stamp. Open Sunday 9 AM–4 PM. Cross Memorial Bridge from Arlington Cemetery and come up Rock Creek Parkway to Georgetown (~15 min). Short towpath walk if time allows. Then walk 5 min to the **Old Stone House**, 3051 M St NW (open Sun 11 AM–7 PM) — Rock Creek Park's Georgetown station, where the **Rock Creek Park stamp + brochure** come from today: the Nature Center drive was dropped to fit Arlington, and NPS's Rock Creek stamp page lists the set at the Old Stone House. **Parking: FREE on Sundays** — DC meters aren't enforced on Sundays, so metered street parking near Thomas Jefferson St / Wisconsin Ave is free today; residential side streets are the backup (permit-zone limits are weekday-only). Skip the paid garages, and always read the sign on the block.
- **1:55 PM — [Kenilworth Park & Aquatic Gardens](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kenilworth+Park+and+Aquatic+Gardens+Washington+DC).** [B] Brochure at the visitor center — also the pickup point for the Anacostia Park brochure. Own stamp. Keep to ~35 min. **Parking: FREE** — the park's own lot at 1550 Anacostia Ave NE; the Kenilworth Park lots nearby are free too. **Stamp gold mine:** per NPS, the Anacostia Park stamp is *only* available here, and this VC also carries Capitol Hill Parks, Civil War Defenses of Washington, Baltimore-Washington Parkway, Potomac Heritage Trail, and National Capital Parks-East stamps — ask for the partner set.
- **2:55 PM — [Belmont-Paul Women's Equality National Monument](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Belmont-Paul+Womens+Equality+National+Monument+144+Constitution+Ave+NE+Washington+DC).** [B] Brochure at the house. [B] Brochure at the visitor center. **Visitor center + fort open Wed–Sun 9 AM–4:30 PM** — a 4:05 arrival is stamp-first — get the set at the desk, then whatever fort walk fits; grounds stay open until sunset (~4:52 PM). ## Know before you go {#know-before-you-go}

- **Bethune Council House is closed for renovation** (NPS, Sep 9, 2026 – Jan 27, 2027) — Day 2's stop there is exterior-only. Its passport stamp is at the Frederick Douglass NHS visitor center (Day 2, 3 PM).

- Check nps.gov for each site the week you go — small sites (Bethune, Woodson, Belmont-Paul, Georgetown C&O) vary by season and staffing. Day 3 stops need Sunday hours confirmed, especially Rock Creek Nature Center.
- Screenshot every ticket before you go — cell service can be spotty.
- Most NPS sites in DC are free. Ford's Theatre, some tours, and parking are the main costs. No entrance fee for the Mall memorials.
- Security: Mall memorials + the White House area have bag checks at some buildings — keep bags small on Day 1.
- Late November on the Mall and Tidal Basin is cold and windy with little shelter — check the forecast the night before each day.
- **Parked for a later add-on (not in this 3-day DC-only trip):** Maryland — Clara Barton NHS, Great Falls Tavern, Fort Washington, Smallwood, Thomas Stone, Glen Echo, Fort Foote, Oxon Cove; Virginia — Arlington House, GW Parkway HQ (Turkey Run), Great Falls VA, Mason Neck / Leesylvania.
